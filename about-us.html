Who we are

Little Hands is a preschool and daycare in Kadavanthra, Kochi. We take children from 8 months old, through playgroup, nursery, LKG and UKG.

Most of the day here is spent playing, because that’s how young children learn best. Through play they learn to talk, share, count, climb and make friends.

Our teachers give each child individual attention, so every child can learn at their own pace and start big school feeling ready.

  • Tuitionup to Class 8
  • Transportoptions available

What we care about

The few things that shape every day at Little Hands.

  • Learning through play

    Songs, stories, blocks and paint. Play is how little ones pick up words, numbers and how to make friends.

  • Individual attention

    Our teachers get to know every child, so each one can learn at their own pace.

  • Safe and welcoming

    A safe place where every child feels included, from their very first day.

  • More than preschool

    Daycare for working parents, tuition for students up to Class 8, and transport options.
