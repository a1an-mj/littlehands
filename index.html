About us

Little Hands is a preschool and daycare on Muttathil Lane, Kadavanthra. Children can join us from 8 months old, and most of their day is spent learning through play.

Our teachers give every child individual attention, so each one can learn at their own pace. We also run tuition for students up to Class 8.
